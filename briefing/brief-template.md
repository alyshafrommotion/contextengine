# Creative Brief Template

A brief is written after an ad concept is finalized and it's ready for production. It has two jobs: document the **context** behind the ad, and give the designer or video editor **instructions** specific enough to produce it without guessing. Every brief takes this structure.

---

## 🧠 Context

[What inspired the idea?]

*The origin story of the brief — why this ad (or set of ads) is getting made in the first place. Keep it short: 2–4 sentences. Just enough to understand why the ad(s) exist in the first place.*

---

## ➡️ Get [audience] who [qualifier] to [action] by [method].

*The strategy of the ad in one sentence. Fill in each slot:*

- ***Audience** — exactly who this ad speaks to*
- ***Qualifier** — the circumstance, status, or emotional state that turns the persona into a micro-persona*
- ***Action** — the specific action we want them to take*
- ***Method** — the actual strategy this ad leverages to get them to take that action*

*If you can't fill all four slots cleanly, the concept is underbaked.*

---

## 🤔 Hypothesis:

[What are we hoping to learn?]

*A falsifiable "if we do X, then Y" statement. If you can't write the hypothesis, the concept isn't ready for a brief yet.*

---

## 💁‍♀️ Audience:

[Who is this ad for? Name the persona or micro-persona.]

*Be exact. If we can't say precisely who is being spoken to in this ad, the ad is not specific enough. Reference the persona/micro-persona by name from the brand's strategy docs, and include the details of their circumstance that the editor needs to keep in mind while producing.*

---

## 🧪 Testing

[What are we testing?]

*What varies across the variations in this brief — and what stays constant. Name the variable clearly so results are readable when the data comes back. If everything varies at once, we learn nothing.*

---

## 📁 Assets to use:

[Link to assets that should be utilized]

*Everything the designer or video editor needs. If anything needs to be generated rather than pulled from existing assets, say so explicitly and describe what to generate.*

---

## 💡 Inspo:

[Include links to visual inspiration]

*Links or embedded files for visual references that nudge the designer or video editor in the right direction.*

---

## ✅ Instructions:

*One numbered block per variation. Give each variation a specific, descriptive name that captures what makes it distinct. Then write detailed production instructions — everything needed to produce it without a follow-up question.*

### 1️⃣ [Variation 1 — specific name]

[Instructions here]

### 2️⃣ [Variation 2 — specific name]

[Instructions here]

### 3️⃣ [Variation 3 — specific name]

[Instructions here]

*(Continue numbering for additional variations.)*
