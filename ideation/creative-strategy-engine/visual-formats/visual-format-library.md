# Visual Format Library

*A visual format is the container an ad comes in: the recognizable shape of the video or static, separate from what it says. This library grows every time you see a format worth keeping.*

**A format is never the message — but it isn't neutral either.** Most formats have a natural job: revealing a problem, proving a transformation, winning a comparison, building trust, closing. That job is what aligns a format with certain stages of awareness — a format that proves is strongest where proof is what the stage demands. A few formats are pure delivery vehicles: they carry any message at any stage, and the awareness stage lives entirely in what's being said. Choosing well means matching the container's natural job to the stage's job — or picking a vehicle and letting the message do the work.

**Stages** use the five stages of awareness: Unaware → Problem-Aware → Solution-Aware → Product-Aware → Most-Aware. Each format lists the range it naturally serves; any format can work outside its range when the messaging is right.

# The formats


## Before and After

Both · Solution-Aware → Most-Aware

**What it is:** Sequential or side-by-side contrast showing the "before/problem" state and the "after/solution" state. The transformation must be visually explicit — shown together, not just described.
**Tells:** Two states of the same subject in one frame or one sequence; the contrast is the content.
**Stage fit:** Most powerful when the audience already knows they have the problem and needs proof that this product solves it.
**Inspo board:** https://projects.motionapp.com/board/6a5468c33efe1d27b747a96c

---


## Billboard

Static · Unaware → Solution-Aware

**What it is:** Creative styled as a large-scale outdoor advertisement — billboard, bus stop, subway wall, highway sign. The ad is shown as if it exists in public space.
**Tells:** The ad appears inside a photo of a public space; the environment is part of the creative.
**Stage fit:** The format signals scale, legitimacy, and mainstream credibility.
**Inspo board:** https://projects.motionapp.com/board/6a5472357073e0800a9341ca

---


## Comment Response

Both · Product-Aware → Most-Aware

**What it is:** Creator directly replies to a surfaced on-screen social comment using the native TikTok/IG reply feature. The format centers on engaging with the comment itself.
**Tells:** The native comment bubble is pinned on screen; the content exists to answer it.
**Stage fit:** Performs best as a retargeting format. Responding to objections, misconceptions, or curiosity signals authenticity.
**Inspo board:** https://projects.motionapp.com/board/6a5471f36a9b9e5432b88d53

---


## Feature Benefit Callout

Static · Product-Aware → Most-Aware

**What it is:** Arrows, labels, or text callouts highlighting 2 or more product features and/or benefits, visually anchored to the product image.
**Tells:** The product is centered with annotations pointing at it.
**Stage fit:** Works when the audience is product-aware and needs to understand what specifically makes this product worth buying.
**Inspo board:** https://projects.motionapp.com/board/6a5468dd07831e5023b092a3

---


## Founder

Video · Unaware → Solution-Aware

**What it is:** The brand's founder or leadership speaks directly about the brand, mission, or product. The founder's role is explicitly stated or strongly implied.
**Tells:** The speaker identifies as the founder or is framed as the person behind the brand.
**Stage fit:** Especially powerful for new customer acquisition where brand trust hasn't been established. Founder stories create immediate human connection.
**Inspo board:** https://projects.motionapp.com/board/6a546c814daa04722ec4ae47

---


## Greenscreen

Video · All stages

**What it is:** A person or subject is cut out and composited onto a separate background — screenshot, website, graphic, or video clip. The speaker appears layered over content rather than filmed naturally in their environment.
**Tells:** The speaker is visibly cut out and floating over content that carries the message.
**Stage fit:** The format is a delivery mechanism, not an awareness signal. Effectiveness depends entirely on what's in the background and what the speaker is saying.
**Inspo board:** https://projects.motionapp.com/board/6a5472483f5467d978d6a502

---


## Letter

Static · Unaware → Solution-Aware

**What it is:** Handwritten or typed long-form note addressed to the viewer. The format mimics a personal letter.
**Tells:** Salutation, body, sign-off — it reads as correspondence, not copy.
**Stage fit:** The personal address creates an intimacy that's disarming. Works especially well for emotional or values-based brands.
**Inspo board:** https://projects.motionapp.com/board/6a5475103efe1d27b747aa31

---


## Native Text Overlay

Static · Unaware → Solution-Aware

**What it is:** Customer or creator photo shot casually (not studio quality) with native-style text overlay as the hook — captions, benefit callouts, or conversational text.
**Tells:** Looks like an organic post; the text overlay is in platform-native styling, not designed type.
**Stage fit:** The casual, in-feed aesthetic reduces ad recognition and lowers scroll resistance.
**Inspo board:** https://projects.motionapp.com/board/6a5468cf7073e0800a9341a5

---


## Podcast

Video · Unaware → Solution-Aware

**What it is:** Multi-mic setup with two or more speakers in a conversational interview or discussion format. Staged and structured.
**Tells:** Visible microphones, seated speakers, conversational back-and-forth.
**Stage fit:** The conversational format feels earned, not sold. Long-form attention with lower ad resistance.
**Inspo board:** https://projects.motionapp.com/board/6a5475651f9548c68a8a47dd

---


## Post It

Both · Unaware → Solution-Aware

**What it is:** Text or message written on a sticky note shown to the camera. The hook is the informal, handwritten delivery.
**Tells:** A physical sticky note carries the hook.
**Stage fit:** Casual format earns attention through its informality.
**Inspo board:** https://projects.motionapp.com/board/6a547838878bd3e6c4386deb

---


## Press

Static · Product-Aware → Most-Aware

**What it is:** Third-party media validation — press logos, headlines, or quotes from reputable outlets (Forbes, Vogue, TechCrunch, etc.).
**Tells:** Recognizable outlet logos or headline styling carry the message.
**Stage fit:** Third-party credibility is most valuable when the audience is already aware of the brand and needs a trust signal to convert.
**Inspo board:** https://projects.motionapp.com/board/6a54758707831e5023b092da

---


## Selfie

Static · Unaware → Solution-Aware

**What it is:** Informal, casual selfie-style image where the creator is the focus. Arm's length, mirror shot, or front-facing casual capture.
**Tells:** Arm's-length or mirror framing; visibly self-shot.
**Stage fit:** The selfie aesthetic reduces ad recognition in-feed.
**Inspo board:** https://projects.motionapp.com/board/6a726dc2ac64005ba8c9cb76

---


## Street Interview

Video · Unaware → Solution-Aware

**What it is:** Vox-pop format — an interviewer asks questions and a responder answers in a public setting (street, store, event). The hook is the impromptu, in-the-wild context.
**Tells:** Handheld mic, public backdrop, unscripted-feeling exchange.
**Stage fit:** Feels earned, not produced. The public setting signals "real people, real opinions."
**Inspo board:** https://projects.motionapp.com/board/6a54723f6a9b9e5432b88d55

---


## Text Message

Static · Unaware → Solution-Aware

**What it is:** SMS or chat-style mockup styled as private or direct messaging.
**Tells:** Chat bubbles, contact header, message-thread layout.
**Stage fit:** The private messaging aesthetic feels unfiltered and personal, reducing ad recognition.
**Inspo board:** https://projects.motionapp.com/board/6a546ecf3efe1d27b747aa13

---


## Us vs. Them

Both · Solution-Aware → Product-Aware

**What it is:** Side-by-side or sequential comparison against a competitor or alternative solution. The comparison is the central focus.
**Tells:** Two options framed against each other; the ad exists to settle the comparison.
**Stage fit:** Most powerful when the audience is product-aware and actively weighing options. Can also work at earlier stages when the comparison is against the "old way" (e.g., this electrolyte vs. plain water) rather than a named competitor.
**Inspo board:** https://projects.motionapp.com/board/6a5468d4301853bbfa191010

---


## VSL (Video Sales Letter)

Video · Product-Aware → Most-Aware

**What it is:** Long-form (typically 60+ seconds), structured, persuasion-driven video that introduces and agitates a problem, builds desire, presents the product as the solution, and drives toward a CTA. Mirrors classic direct-response sales letter structure.
**Tells:** Runs long, follows a visible problem → agitation → solution → proof → CTA arc.
**Stage fit:** Designed for audiences close to purchasing who need a complete, persuasive argument to convert.
**Inspo board:** https://projects.motionapp.com/board/6a726d7157dd7e808728dbc0

---


## Yapper

Video · All stages

**What it is:** Single-take, direct-to-camera talking-head video. One speaker, one environment, one unbroken performance — like they propped up their phone and started talking. Minor jump cuts acceptable only if same space, same speaker, no new footage introduced.
**Tells:** One person, one location, no b-roll, no scene changes.
**Stage fit:** The format is a delivery vehicle, not an awareness signal. Awareness stage depends entirely on what the Yapper is saying and how they're saying it.
**Inspo board:** https://projects.motionapp.com/board/6a546c84580297ce65a75067

# How to add a format

Give the LLM a format you like and tell it why you like it — from a description, or from a link to a reel, TikTok, or video.

1. Add the format's entry to the formats section using the template below, in alphabetical order
2. Save any source links in the entry's examples table, so they can be revisited
3. Add one row to the index table

## Entry template

```
## [Format Name]

[Video / Static / Both] · [Stage range]

**What it is:** [what defines the container]
**Tells:** [how you recognize it in the wild]
**Stage fit:** [why it serves that range]
**Inspo board:** [link]
```
